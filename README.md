## How I Approach Engineering

- **Start with the problem:** understand users, constraints, risks, and success criteria before selecting technology.
- **Design for operations:** prioritize maintainability, observability, clear interfaces, and predictable failure behavior.
- **Build with security in mind:** favor least privilege, secure defaults, and explicit trust boundaries.
- **Automate thoughtfully:** remove repetitive work while retaining control, traceability, and safeguards.
- **Measure outcomes:** validate performance and business impact with evidence rather than assumptions.
- **Document decisions:** make systems easier for others to understand, operate, and improve.
